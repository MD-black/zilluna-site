# zilluna.com — coming soon

The page zilluna.com shows until the Zilluna app is hosted: the logo on the app's morning sky, «كل ما يحتاجه طفلك في مكان واحد», and two ways to reach us (the Facebook page and info@zilluna.com).

One static page, served by GitHub Pages from `main` with the custom domain in `CNAME`. The logo, icons and cover image come from the app's brand files (`docs/brand` in the app's repository); the two typefaces are the app's own, Baloo Bhaijaan 2 and Rubik, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/`). No scripts, no cookies, no outside requests.

When the app goes live, zilluna.com points to it instead and this repository can be archived.
